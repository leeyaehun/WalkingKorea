# Bukchon main street — Step 04: paving, terminal edges and flickering stone foundations

2026-11-01 | Three.js / WalkingKorea | cloud deployment

## Specific reported defect: grey foundation changes colour as the camera moves

A real coplanar surface conflict was confirmed by code review in
`BukchonFrontage.js`: the original scenic-house joining walls rendered a
**full-height plaster quad** first, then another **stone foundation quad**
over the same plane from grade (-0.25m) to +0.35m. Both shared a depth,
therefore their material result could alternate from camera angle to camera
angle (classic Z-fighting). The fix splits the facade: granite below
+0.35m, plaster only ABOVE +0.35m; they share an edge, not an area.

Also, Step 03's longer infill wall generator independently re-drew walls
already generated by the old adjacent-house connector builder. It now
receives all existing connector metadata and excludes those longitudinal
intervals, preserving the old masonry with no duplicate flush panels.
The combined existing+new visual wall coverage exceeds 48m; the number
of **new** wall polygons decreases by design.

Both defects are now covered by geometry-level tests that check separated
vertical ranges and absence of overlapping reserved connector intervals.
This is not merely changing a shader colour or adding a polygon offset.

## Main street finishing

- Paving elevations separated: grout .023m, asphalt .077m, narrow kerb
  .090m, drain metal .103m, iron grate .111m. Previously the separation
  was mostly 0.002–0.025m and could shimmer at grazing angles.
- Decorative patches are raised .013m above asphalt (but are sparse).
- ~198m of thin pale stone pedestrian edge treatment (both sides of the
  ~99m main alley) now bridges the pale kerb to just before house walls.
  No new trafficable area or playable side street; the actual movement
  collision / minimap / six POIs remain one route.
- Two short **stone end thresholds** beyond the asphalt terminuses
  stop the route from hard-cutting straight into tan terrain. These are
  non-playable visual end caps, not a collision extension.
- The 8m checkerboard-style sand/grey ground plates are replaced with
  smaller, contiguous 5m neutral stone ground triangles following the
  same terrain hill. The artwork should no longer read as patchwork beige
  undeveloped squares behind/at the wall feet.
- All original 20 houses, wall gate, existing lights, shadow map, female
  sprite walking, night transition and distant Namsan/Seoul backdrop stay.

## Validation

`tests/bukchon-main-street-step04.test.js` checks coplanar wall bands,
deduplicated walls on both sides, the correct ordering of paving layers,
two road terminus aprons, finite one-mesh geometry, limits and no vegetation.
After merge, the Cloudflare build must pass all Node tests and Vite bundle
before browser screenshots at default day, Namsan and night views.
